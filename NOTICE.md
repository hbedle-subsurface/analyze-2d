## Seismic data

**`data/SCAN029_full_stack.sgy`**

SCAN line L2EBN2020ASCAN029, pre-stack time migrated full stack. Acquired and
processed within SCAN (Seismische Campagne Aardwarmte Nederland), the geothermal
exploration programme carried out jointly by EBN and TNO since 2018. The final
seismic sections, velocities and processing reports of the SCAN lines are
published for download on NLOG, the Dutch oil, gas and geothermal portal run by
TNO: <https://www.nlog.nl/en/scan-2d-seismic-data>.

The copy in this repository has been reduced for use in a browser: every second
trace of a 10 m display grid (20 m trace spacing), 0.15 to 3.0 s at 2 ms,
IEEE float. How it was made is recorded in its textual header and in
`data/README.md`. Processing, acquisition and any interpretation of the line
remain the work of EBN, TNO and their contractors, and anyone reusing the data
should cite SCAN and NLOG as the source and check the current NLOG terms of use.
