Attributes

Tick the attributes to compute and press Show. Each one is drawn under the section on the same axes, and the table below the panels gives the correlation between every pair.

Co-rendering

At 1 each panel shows the attribute alone. Below 1 the attribute is drawn over the seismic amplitude, so the reflections show through it. The color map of each panel is chosen on the panel.

Relief shading

Relief is solved for 2% of the image in full shadow until the slider is moved. These settings apply to relief shading and relief over amplitude.
