Self-organizing map

Choose the attributes and the size of the map, then train it. The run is read on the SHAP page, and a second map is trained and compared with it on the Refine and compare page.

Facies window

Attributes that describe a level are averaged over this window; attributes that oscillate with the wavelet are used as computed.

Analysis window

With "Drag to set the window" chosen above the section, a box dragged on it sets the window. Attributes are standardized within the window, and only samples inside it are trained on and classified.

Runs

Each square is one neuron; the dot size gives how many samples it took. Click a neuron to hide its class, shift-click to show it alone.
